# 🟦 Cubelight Launcher

**Cubelight Launcher** is a powerful, lightweight, and offline-friendly custom Minecraft launcher designed to give players full control over their game files, profiles, and modding experience. 

Built with a focus on data preservation and single-player stability, Cubelight Launcher ensures your worlds, mods, and configurations remain safely stored on your local machine—even if you lose access to your online account.

---

## ✨ Key Features

* **🔒 Offline & Local First:** Play seamlessly without an internet connection. Your worlds, screenshots, and mod configurations are strictly saved locally to protect your data from account loss.
* **👤 Account Modes:** 
  * **Local Profiles:** Fully supported for complete offline independence.
  * **Microsoft / Xbox Sign-In:** Available exclusively for users who own a legitimate Microsoft account containing the game. *(Note: The long-term stability and current status of Microsoft authentication within this custom environment are currently unknown/experimental).*
* **📦 Built-in Mod Manager:** Integrated support for searching, downloading, and updating mods directly from **CurseForge** and **Modrinth**.
* **⚙️ Dynamic Dependency Management:** Automatically handles library requirements (like `Cloth Config`, `YACL`, and Forge/Fabric dependencies).
* **🖥️ Clean & Intuitive UI:** Modern sidebar navigation with quick access to game settings, save backups, and launch configurations.

## ⚠️ Known Issues & Upcoming Fixes

We believe in full transparency with our users. Here are the current known issues being worked on:

* **Modpack Visibility Issue:** When downloading a complete modpack, it may temporarily not appear in the instance menu immediately after download. 
  * *Status:* **Fix in progress.** This is purely a UI display refresh delay and will be completely resolved in the upcoming patch. All mod files remain safe on your local drive.

## 🚀 Getting Started

1. Download the latest release from the [Releases](https://github.com/YOUR_USERNAME/Cubelight-Launcher/releases) section.
2. Launch `Cubelight Launcher`.
3. Choose **Create Local Profile** to jump straight into offline gameplay, or test the Microsoft sign-in option.
4. Select your desired Minecraft version, install your favorite mods, and launch the game!
